# CareerCoachAI — Shot-by-Shot Video Storyboard
## Duration: 3 Minutes 30 Seconds

---

### Shot 1: The Problem & Vision (0:00 - 0:35)
- **Visual:** Split screen showing resume rejection notification vs. live CareerCoachAI hero landing page.
- **Camera Framing:** Wide browser capture at 1080p, 60fps.
- **Audio Cue:** Clear, upbeat narration introducing the platform.
- **Action:** Smooth scroll over key value propositions.

### Shot 2: Resume Intelligence Studio (0:35 - 1:15)
- **Visual:** `/resume` dropzone with sample PDF resume drag-and-drop.
- **Camera Framing:** Screen focus on ATS score gauge (84/100) and capability breakdown.
- **Action:** Hover over extracted skills and capability gaps cards.

### Shot 3: Communication & Speech Coach (1:15 - 1:55)
- **Visual:** `/communication` microphone widget active, recording waveform.
- **Camera Framing:** Close-up on WPM speedometer and filler-word detection badges.
- **Action:** Speaker delivers answer with intentional filler words (`um`, `like`), showing instant detection.

### Shot 4: Interactive AI Mock Interview Studio (1:55 - 2:45)
- **Visual:** `/interview` with webcam enabled and MediaPipe computer vision HUD overlay.
- **Camera Framing:** Split view showing candidate webcam with face centering HUD on the right, question prompt on the left.
- **Action:** Deliver answer, click submit, and reveal STAR checkmarks (Situation, Task, Action, Result) with 88/100 composite score.

### Shot 5: Gamified Economy & Readiness Dashboard (2:45 - 3:15)
- **Visual:** `/dashboard` Career Readiness radar chart and `/wallet` coin transaction ledger.
- **Action:** Show coin rewards popping up after interview completion.

### Shot 6: Architecture & Closing Pitch (3:15 - 3:30)
- **Visual:** Full-screen vector architecture diagram with live production URLs.
- **Action:** Thank judges and invite questions.
