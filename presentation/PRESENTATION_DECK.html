THE CHALLENGE

The Broken Hiring Pipeline

Job seekers face automated rejections, expensive human coaching barriers, and a total vacuum of actionable feedback.

$200+/Hour Coaching

Unstructured Delivery

The Feedback Desert

Full-Stack Architecture & Privacy-First AI

A decoupled, high-resilience architecture with zero mock AI fallbacks and client-side computer vision.
Client-Side Vision (WASM)

MediaPipe FaceLandmarker runs in-browser on GPU/WASM. Evaluates face centering & eye engagement. Zero video uploaded.

Gemini Multimodal API

Live Express backend sends audio and text to Google Gemini Flash with strict JSON schema enforcement. No hallucinations.

Hybrid Persistence Tier

MongoDB Atlas Mongoose integration with automated fallback to local salted scrypt encrypted storage in development.

Interview Studio: answers that follow STAR

  • Choose a role and level: Entry, Mid or Senior
  • Five behavioural & technical questions synthesized dynamically
  • Answer by typing, microphone or video preview with live HUD
  • Scored for STAR completeness, relevance, clarity and correctness
  • Post-session review of strengths and actionable improvements

Live transcription uses the browser's Web Speech API and Gemini multimodal audio. Each session saves its transcript, pace, filler count, clarity and tone scores, with actionable feedback.

Resume Studio: from PDF to a clear to-do list

  1. 1. Upload a PDF resume (verified with magic byte checks)
  2. 2. Seven core sections are checked for completeness
  3. 3. Evidence-grounded ATS score from 0 to 100 is generated
  4. 4. Paste a target job title and job description
  5. 5. See matching and missing keywords, with concrete gap closing roadmaps
Zero hallucination: Gemini is strictly constrained to extract only verified facts present in the uploaded document.
Sections checked
Contact Summary Education Experience Skills Projects Certifications
Matching uses keyword overlap and competency grounding between the resume and the target job description.
